# 02 — Building Rapportive

Covers `5:46`–`10:47`. The second startup, Y Combinator, and the LinkedIn acquisition.

## The product

- Rapportive (~2010) put social media inside Gmail: a browser extension that manipulated the Gmail web UI to show, next to each email, a social profile of the sender — photo and job title from LinkedIn, recent tweets from Twitter, Facebook posts, whatever could be found. [`5:46`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SVOrURyOu_U&t=346) [`6:14`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SVOrURyOu_U&t=374)
- It quickly became popular, which enabled raising money from a still-young Y Combinator; the team moved from the UK to San Francisco for the program and then stayed on US work visas. [`6:14`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SVOrURyOu_U&t=374) [`6:38`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SVOrURyOu_U&t=398)
- The Bay Area proved very open to outsiders: starting with one or two contacts, introductions compounded into a real network and an established position. [`7:30`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SVOrURyOu_U&t=450)

## The acquisition (2012) — the honest version

- Sold to LinkedIn in 2012 with a team of just five people; a success for everyone involved, but not a huge sum. [`7:57`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SVOrURyOu_U&t=477) [`8:11`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SVOrURyOu_U&t=491)
- The real context: they were burning money, user growth was okay but not enough to raise a big round, revenue attempts hadn't worked — so it was sell or shut down. They couldn't even reduce their own salaries because that would have violated their visa conditions. [`8:11`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SVOrURyOu_U&t=491) [`8:37`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SVOrURyOu_U&t=517) [`9:10`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SVOrURyOu_U&t=550)
- Selling was "the least bad option", and given the lack of leverage Martin is happy with the outcome: LinkedIn let the team stay together and operate as an independent unit inside the company. [`9:37`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SVOrURyOu_U&t=577)

## Aftermath inside LinkedIn

- Rapportive itself went on life support; the team built a new product released as **LinkedIn Intro**, which got a weird reception and was shut down shortly after launch. The team was then disbanded, but Martin credits LinkedIn for the freedom they were given throughout. [`10:03`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SVOrURyOu_U&t=603) [`10:36`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SVOrURyOu_U&t=636)
