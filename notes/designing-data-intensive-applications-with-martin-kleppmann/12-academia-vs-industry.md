# 12 — Academia vs. industry

Covers `1:00:12`–`1:03:50`. What academics actually do, and why Martin values the academic vantage point.

## The range within academia

- Academia isn't one thing: some researchers are fully theoretical/mathematical, uninterested in the real world; others are applied, aiming for real-world impact. Martin is at the applied end. [`1:00:30`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SVOrURyOu_U&t=3630) [`1:00:57`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SVOrURyOu_U&t=3657)

## The key difference: time horizon

- Startups must ship within months and can't afford to think 10 years out. Big-company infrastructure teams can think somewhat longer because requirements are better understood. Academia can think genuinely long-term, on problems not immediately commercially viable — or **misaligned with commercial incentives entirely**. [`1:00:57`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SVOrURyOu_U&t=3657) [`1:01:21`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SVOrURyOu_U&t=3681) [`1:01:46`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SVOrURyOu_U&t=3706)

## Example: local-first software as a counter-incentive agenda

- Local-first software: take some power away from cloud operators and give it back to end users — users more in control of their data, less dependent on cloud services. [`1:01:46`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SVOrURyOu_U&t=3706)
- Why companies won't do it: SaaS subscriptions work because the vendor can "hold a gun to the customer's head — pay us, otherwise we delete all your data". Removing that gun undermines the very lock-in the revenue depends on. [`1:02:37`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SVOrURyOu_U&t=3757) [`1:02:58`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SVOrURyOu_U&t=3778)
- In academia Martin can prioritize what's right for users over companies' commercial models — while working on genuinely interesting engineering and CS problems. [`1:02:58`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SVOrURyOu_U&t=3778) [`1:03:31`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SVOrURyOu_U&t=3811)
