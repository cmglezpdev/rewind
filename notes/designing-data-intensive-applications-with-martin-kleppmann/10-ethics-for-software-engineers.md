# 10 — Ethics for software engineers

Covers `49:02`–`52:45`. "Doing the Right Thing" — a subsection in the first edition, now a full closing chapter in the second.

## Why the chapter exists

- Book quote: "We, the engineers building these systems, have a responsibility to carefully consider those consequences and consciously decide what kind of world we want to live in." [`49:02`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SVOrURyOu_U&t=2942)
- Motivation: ethics was ignored during Martin's time in industry — especially in startups, where product focus deprioritized ethical questions; consumer products were often geared toward behavioral data harvesting because that's what advertising monetizes, with very little reflection on good and bad. [`49:02`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SVOrURyOu_U&t=2942) [`49:31`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SVOrURyOu_U&t=2971)
- The chapter doesn't prescribe an approach; it points out that data protection legislation now exists and must be considered in data architecture, and that ethical responsibility is real. "If you want to change the world, thinking about the impact your technologies have on the world is part of your job." [`50:00`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SVOrURyOu_U&t=3000) [`50:30`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SVOrURyOu_U&t=3030)
- It also reflects Martin's own process: he didn't think about ethics early on either; writing the section was his way of grappling with it. [`50:58`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SVOrURyOu_U&t=3058)

## Engineers' leverage

- Engineers are in a uniquely strong position to influence these systems — arguably as strong a voice as regulators will have years later. [`51:35`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SVOrURyOu_U&t=3095)
- Practical framing: engineers articulate trade-offs so business leaders can make educated decisions; the risks in those trade-offs include not just technical risks (data corruption) but **societal risks** — harms, unintended consequences, and reputational damage. The ask: make intentional, deliberate decisions instead of sweeping it under the carpet. [`51:56`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SVOrURyOu_U&t=3116) [`52:21`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SVOrURyOu_U&t=3141)
