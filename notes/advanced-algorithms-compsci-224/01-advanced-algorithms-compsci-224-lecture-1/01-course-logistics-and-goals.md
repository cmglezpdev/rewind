## Rules that shape the work

- **No textbook.** Students take turns scribing lecture notes in LaTeX from a website template; lectures are recorded so you can fill gaps [`1:59`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0JUN9aDxVmI&t=119), [`2:16`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0JUN9aDxVmI&t=136).
- One person scribes each lecture; notes are due **9:00 PM the following day**, and the instructor may edit them afterward [`5:05`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0JUN9aDxVmI&t=305), [`5:20`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0JUN9aDxVmI&t=320), [`5:59`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0JUN9aDxVmI&t=359).
- Scribe notes must carry **references** (conference/journal + year), which is why the lecturer states them on the board [`19:36`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0JUN9aDxVmI&t=1176), [`20:23`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0JUN9aDxVmI&t=1223).
- Final project: proposal around **October 30**, then roughly six weeks of work; the instructor reads proposals and gives feedback [`2:44`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0JUN9aDxVmI&t=164), [`3:26`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0JUN9aDxVmI&t=206).
- P-sets are LaTeXed, emailed, and have **page limits** — deliberately, to discourage padding when you don't know an answer; brief solutions are appreciated [`3:26`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0JUN9aDxVmI&t=206), [`3:59`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0JUN9aDxVmI&t=239).
- Every student must **grade at least once**: teams of 3–4 students plus the TF meet once per P-set. Scribing and grading slots are first come, first served [`4:19`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0JUN9aDxVmI&t=259), [`4:36`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0JUN9aDxVmI&t=276).

## CS124 vs. CS224

- Algorithms is broad: a full semester of CS124 does not cover all topics [`6:50`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0JUN9aDxVmI&t=410).
- CS224 adds **new models and new measures of efficiency** not seen in 124, and is more theory-focused — no programming assignments (an implementation option exists only for the final project) [`7:12`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0JUN9aDxVmI&t=432), [`7:29`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0JUN9aDxVmI&t=449).

## Stated goals

1. Increase ability to **analyze** and **create** algorithms via many techniques absent from 124 [`7:29`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0JUN9aDxVmI&t=449).
2. **Modeling**: draw inspiration for models and study which model you analyze an algorithm in [`8:17`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0JUN9aDxVmI&t=497).
3. Go beyond CS124's informal "running time = number of steps" plus memory, to other cost parameters [`8:53`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0JUN9aDxVmI&t=533), [`9:06`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0JUN9aDxVmI&t=546).
