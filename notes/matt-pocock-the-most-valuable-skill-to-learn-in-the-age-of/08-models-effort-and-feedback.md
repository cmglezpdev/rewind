## The concrete improvement to his video editor

- Previously, the agent could not reliably run and inspect the application. Local OBS and other third-party dependencies complicated access while Pocock was also recording with it. [`33:02`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Z8lf5oWKfj0&t=1982) [`33:29`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Z8lf5oWKfj0&t=2009)
- After fixing the setup, the agent could click through the application, capture screenshots, and run it properly. Pocock reports a large improvement in output quality because the agent had better evidence about the application. No numerical quality gain is given. [`33:29`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Z8lf5oWKfj0&t=2009)
- He prioritizes improving this working environment over continually comparing models. His suggestion that an older model could perform similarly with the improved setup is a hypothesis, not a demonstrated comparison.
