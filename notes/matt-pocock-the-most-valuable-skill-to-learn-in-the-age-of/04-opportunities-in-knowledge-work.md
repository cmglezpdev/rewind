# Opportunities in knowledge work

## Build skills that transfer between career paths

- Asked to choose between consulting and advancing within an employer, Pocock focuses on transferable agent skills that can support either path. [`12:09`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Z8lf5oWKfj0&t=729) [`12:27`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Z8lf5oWKfj0&t=747)
- He sees teaching and adoption support as opportunities because organizations he speaks with struggle to adopt AI. He also acknowledges ethical concerns rather than treating adoption as uncontroversial. [`10:33`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Z8lf5oWKfj0&t=633) [`11:01`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Z8lf5oWKfj0&t=661)
- Other possibilities he names include applications that embed AI and helping companies improve their existing systems. These are suggested opportunities, not evidence of guaranteed business demand. [`11:21`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Z8lf5oWKfj0&t=681)

## The difficult skills

- Decide which outputs to trust and how to organize agents. Stronger models do not automatically remove the learning curve. [`12:52`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Z8lf5oWKfj0&t=772) [`13:10`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Z8lf5oWKfj0&t=790)
- Control the information agents receive, including its format and data structures. [`13:10`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Z8lf5oWKfj0&t=790)
- Turn human processes into instructions agents can follow, then make those instructions reusable. [`13:30`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Z8lf5oWKfj0&t=810)
- Design ways to verify the work. Pocock argues that coding and mathematical proofs offer clearer verification than much everyday knowledge work. [`13:30`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Z8lf5oWKfj0&t=810)

## Where Pocock sees the strongest opportunity

- Adapt agents to an organization's particular knowledge-work processes. He says finding a workable process for his own daily work took many attempts and still requires improvement. This difficulty is why he sees organizational process design as valuable. [`14:07`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Z8lf5oWKfj0&t=847) [`14:40`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Z8lf5oWKfj0&t=880)
