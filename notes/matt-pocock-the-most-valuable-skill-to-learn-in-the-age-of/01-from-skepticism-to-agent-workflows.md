# From skepticism to agent workflows

## The shift in Pocock's work

- Pocock describes roughly ten years of coding experience, including about eight professionally, before his move to agent-led development. He identifies the period around Opus 4.5 and December 2025 as his personal turning point. [`1:50`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Z8lf5oWKfj0&t=110)
- His earlier interest in teaching colleagues and improving their working environment transferred to helping agents produce better work. The change was in who performed the coding; his experience shaping the development process remained useful. [`2:46`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Z8lf5oWKfj0&t=166) [`3:12`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Z8lf5oWKfj0&t=192)
- The interview's central recommendation is to tell agents what you care about. A more capable model still lacks your specific intentions and priorities. [`4:24`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Z8lf5oWKfj0&t=264)
