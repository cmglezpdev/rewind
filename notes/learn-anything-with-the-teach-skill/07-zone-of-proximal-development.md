# 7. The zone of proximal development

The key teaching idea behind the whole skill — the one Matt says he is "obsessed with" ([`7:16`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=s5T5oQJcJ6U&t=436)).

## Definition

- **Always teach someone in the area where they are perfectly challenged but not intimidated** ([`7:16`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=s5T5oQJcJ6U&t=436)).
- The student should be **neither bored nor freaked out** ([`7:16`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=s5T5oQJcJ6U&t=436)).

## What it forces on lesson design

Every lesson must be ([`7:16`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=s5T5oQJcJ6U&t=436)):

- concise
- compact
- **exactly framed** at the learner's current zone

And, per the demo, short, really focused, and telling the learner **exactly what to practise now** ([`8:04`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=s5T5oQJcJ6U&t=484)).

## The corner-cycle lesson as evidence

- The generated lesson breaks the algorithm down and supplies **new mental models** rather than repeating the old explanation ([`7:41`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=s5T5oQJcJ6U&t=461)).
- The reframing that landed: the algorithm is **one four-move phrase played twice** ([`7:41`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=s5T5oQJcJ6U&t=461)).
- The algorithm is read aloud move by move; the auto-transcription garbles it, so treat the exact sequence in the transcript as unreliable and re-check it in the video ([`7:41`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=s5T5oQJcJ6U&t=461)).

## Interactivity — the payoff of HTML

- The lesson shipped an **interactive tap-through** of the algorithm ([`7:41`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=s5T5oQJcJ6U&t=461)).
- It also has a **guided mode** that can be toggled off — his reaction: "unreasonably cool" ([`8:04`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=s5T5oQJcJ6U&t=484)).
- The point being made: HTML lessons give you **the full power of the browser** to build feedback loops that Markdown could never provide ([`8:04`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=s5T5oQJcJ6U&t=484)).

## Why statefulness and the ZPD are the same feature

- Everything is saved to the file system, so after clearing the context, **every `teach` run has the context it needs to keep the learner in the zone of proximal development** ([`8:04`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=s5T5oQJcJ6U&t=484)).
