# Skatebench, vision, and evaluation scope

## Private skateboard-trick naming results

| Model as named in the transcript | Reported result |
| --- | --- |
| Gemini 4 Argan | 100%, first perfect score on Theo's private Skatebench. [`7:43`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NE006pkiAnk&t=463) |
| Opus 5.5 Max | 79%. [`8:14`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NE006pkiAnk&t=494) |
| "3.8 Flash" | 96%; name preserved from the transcript. [`8:14`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NE006pkiAnk&t=494) |

- Skatebench tests naming skateboard tricks, including switch and fakie stances. Theo describes these stance distinctions as a grammar that makes the task difficult for models. [`8:14`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NE006pkiAnk&t=494)
- Gemini answered quickly without excessive reasoning-token use on this task, unlike his broader experience of the model's token consumption. [`8:53`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NE006pkiAnk&t=533)
- Early API instability required changes to his benchmark runner to handle endpoint errors. He does not know whether reliability improved after that testing. [`8:33`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NE006pkiAnk&t=513)
- His lesson is task specificity: a perfect result is meaningful for comparable work, but does not establish reliability on unrelated tasks. [`8:53`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NE006pkiAnk&t=533)

## Vision strengths and untested possibilities

- Theo describes historical Gemini strengths with documents, PDFs, video, audio, and image understanding, giving motherboard-component labeling and overlays as an example. He explicitly did not benchmark those broader vision tasks for this review. [`9:20`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NE006pkiAnk&t=560) [`9:47`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NE006pkiAnk&t=587)
- He suggests evaluating Gemini on complex vision work rather than assuming his coding verdict transfers to those tasks. [`9:47`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NE006pkiAnk&t=587)
- He has heard positive reports about internationalization, localization, and multilingual work, but has not seen much of that capability in his own tests. These remain suggestions for further evaluation. [`16:10`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NE006pkiAnk&t=970) [`16:37`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NE006pkiAnk&t=997)
