# Release context, disclosures, and Google's claims

## What is being reviewed

- Theo describes Gemini 4 Argan as Google's first large Pro-like model release in eight months, following Gemini 3.1 Pro in February. He contrasts that interval with the one-to-two-month gap between the competing releases he names. [`0:00`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NE006pkiAnk&t=0)
- The review compares pricing, front-end output, 3D games, specialized capabilities, and code work against Opus 5.5, 6.1 Soul, Astra, and Muse variants. [`0:22`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NE006pkiAnk&t=22)
- Google contacts arranged early access. Theo says Google did not pay for this Gemini or Antigravity coverage, while disclosing that YouTube pays him as a creator. [`0:51`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NE006pkiAnk&t=51) [`1:09`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NE006pkiAnk&t=69)

## Availability and vendor evidence

- The launch announcement lists API, Studio, and Gemini-platform access for developers and enterprises; Ultra access in Antigravity; and Ultra/Pro access in the Gemini app and AI Mode in Search. This is availability as described in the video. [`2:38`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NE006pkiAnk&t=158)
- Google claims a number-one ranking on the "VL index" across economic domains. Theo challenges the comparison's value calculation because it assigns Muse Wonderful a token price he says was not available. [`3:09`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NE006pkiAnk&t=189) [`3:37`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NE006pkiAnk&t=217)
- Google reports over 35% more agentic code submissions and nearly one-third fewer rollbacks. Its examples include a refactor across hundreds of files completed in weeks rather than months and Python optimization in hours. These are vendor claims, not Theo's reproduced tests. [`5:45`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NE006pkiAnk&t=345) [`6:40`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NE006pkiAnk&t=400)
- A customer testimonial claims 100% completion on long-running workflows. Theo's objection is methodological: that percentage means little without knowing the tasks and their relevance to a user's work. [`7:12`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NE006pkiAnk&t=432) [`7:43`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NE006pkiAnk&t=463)

## Sponsor segment

- WorkOS sponsors the video. The promoted standard, transcribed as "OMD," lets agents register for applications on a user's behalf; Theo names Firecrawl and Cloudflare as partners and Neon, Monday, and Parallel as adopters. This is sponsor material, separate from Gemini testing.
