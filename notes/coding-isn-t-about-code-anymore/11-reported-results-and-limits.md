# Reported results and limits of the argument

## The result Theo presents

- Theo shows a chart of T3 Code PRs merged per day. He reports an earlier range of about 2–20 daily merges, compared with roughly 60 on a busy weekend day and more than 400 on active days. [`43:05`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GtJKFhztgdM&t=2585) [`43:31`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GtJKFhztgdM&t=2611)
- He attributes this rise to maintaining a healthy codebase for agents. The transcript provides no controlled comparison or breakdown of other causes, so this supports his account of the team's experience rather than establishing gardening as the sole cause. [`43:31`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GtJKFhztgdM&t=2611)

## What to retain

- His gardening argument connects older platform practice with agent development: identify friction, improve the environment, watch contributions, and remove causes of repeated mistakes. The documentation repair, test cleanup, and transfer-size action are concrete instances. [`13:01`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GtJKFhztgdM&t=781) [`39:20`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GtJKFhztgdM&t=2360) [`40:55`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GtJKFhztgdM&t=2455)
- The aim is continued product delivery. Platform work earns its value through what contributors can ship afterward, as illustrated by the Convex migration and his concluding PR activity figures. [`28:39`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GtJKFhztgdM&t=1719) [`28:55`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GtJKFhztgdM&t=1735) [`43:31`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GtJKFhztgdM&t=2611)
- Theo acknowledges that some of his current maintenance techniques are working well while he is still unsure about others. The video offers a working philosophy and examples, rather than a complete validated methodology. [`44:43`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GtJKFhztgdM&t=2683)
