# The Wayfinder skill

- Wayfinder extends the planning idea when a single grilling session cannot cover the whole problem. [`45:02`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4DhcSPkEbwI&t=2702)
- Matt designed it for work too large to plan inside about 150,000 tokens, such as an entire Stripe clone. [`45:02`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4DhcSPkEbwI&t=2702)
- The skill centers on a "map" that records decisions, open questions, and the current path toward the destination. [`45:25`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4DhcSPkEbwI&t=2725)
- Each grilling session reveals more of the map, reducing the "fog of war" around the project. [`45:58`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4DhcSPkEbwI&t=2758)
- Wayfinder can include different ticket types, including grilling, prototyping, research, and infrastructure tasks. [`46:45`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4DhcSPkEbwI&t=2805)
- Matt has used maps with 50 to 100 tickets before reaching the destination. [`46:45`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4DhcSPkEbwI&t=2805)
- He also uses Wayfinder outside code, including course planning and planning a garden office. [`47:11`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4DhcSPkEbwI&t=2831)
- The broader claim is that engineering-like planning skills can transfer to other domains where people make decisions, gather information, and act. [`47:11`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4DhcSPkEbwI&t=2831)
