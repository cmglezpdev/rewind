# Joining Vercel

- Matt joined Vercel's developer education orbit when Lee Robinson was leading that work and Jared Palmer was involved in his team. [`13:00`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4DhcSPkEbwI&t=780)
- He was already thinking about TypeScript education before joining Vercel. [`13:23`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4DhcSPkEbwI&t=803)
- His desire to teach came from missing the teaching work he had done before becoming a developer. [`14:01`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4DhcSPkEbwI&t=841)
- Advanced TypeScript became his topic because making XState type-safe exposed him to difficult type-system patterns. [`14:23`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4DhcSPkEbwI&t=863)
- Short TypeScript tips on Twitter grew his audience from roughly 4,000 to 10,000 followers. [`14:52`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4DhcSPkEbwI&t=892)
- Matt took a three-day-a-week Vercel contract so he could keep testing the TypeScript course idea. [`15:18`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4DhcSPkEbwI&t=918)
- He worked at Vercel during the Turbopack launch and helped write initial documentation. [`17:07`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4DhcSPkEbwI&t=1027)
- A pre-release sale for Total TypeScript earned roughly 30 to 40 times his Vercel compensation, making the course the clear choice. [`17:51`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4DhcSPkEbwI&t=1071)
