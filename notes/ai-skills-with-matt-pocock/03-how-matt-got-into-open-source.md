# How Matt got into open source

- Matt was experimenting with open source on the side and paid attention to developers he wanted to learn from, including David Khourshid. [`10:16`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4DhcSPkEbwI&t=616)
- A complex real-time application led him to use XState for state machines across a network boundary. [`10:40`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4DhcSPkEbwI&t=640)
- XState worked well enough that Matt started building type-safety tooling around it. [`11:06`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4DhcSPkEbwI&t=666)
- That tooling brought him into the XState core team, where he contributed issues and took part in design discussions. [`11:06`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4DhcSPkEbwI&t=666)
- Working with the XState team exposed him to a level of software design skill he had not seen before. [`11:27`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4DhcSPkEbwI&t=687)
- Joining the XState company gave him his first job paid at US-market compensation, which changed how he thought about money, flexibility, and working on something he cared about. [`11:52`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4DhcSPkEbwI&t=712)
- Advocacy around state charts and XState helped get him noticed by people at Vercel. [`12:34`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4DhcSPkEbwI&t=754)
