# Getting into tech

How Navi (NeetCode) went from electrical engineering student to falling in love with programming.

- Studied electrical engineering in college because he liked math and physics; took the required intro to C class reluctantly [`6:43`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xafwfGVBxos&t=403)
- Was initially bad at it — struggled with `printf` format specifiers while everyone around him picked it up quickly; programming felt like "a very different way of thinking" despite its relation to math [`7:03`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xafwfGVBxos&t=423)
- It clicked after learning variables, conditions, loops, and functions: simple primitives compose into "infinite complexity" — like Google Spanner combining programming with physics, atomic clocks, and GPS [`7:30`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xafwfGVBxos&t=450), [`7:55`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xafwfGVBxos&t=475)
- Reality check on entering the workforce: programming is a business — you don't get to pick the languages or problems you enjoy, which "took some of the fun out of it" and left him with a love-hate relationship with programming [`8:18`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xafwfGVBxos&t=498), [`8:37`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xafwfGVBxos&t=517)
