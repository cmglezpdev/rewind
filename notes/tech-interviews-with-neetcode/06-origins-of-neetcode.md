# The origins of NeetCode

NeetCode started as free tutorial videos made "for the love of the game" after quitting Amazon — roughly 6 years before this interview.

- In the gap year between Amazon and Google, Navi recorded LeetCode explanation videos: "I'm studying this right now… I might as well help some other people" [`22:26`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xafwfGVBxos&t=1346), [`22:52`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xafwfGVBxos&t=1372)
- At the time there were no real tutorials — just forum posts with complex solutions most people memorized at a high level without understanding why they worked [`22:52`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xafwfGVBxos&t=1372), [`23:16`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xafwfGVBxos&t=1396)
- His "deep thinking" trait made him go far deeper than seemed rational: hours per video for ~50 viewers [`23:16`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xafwfGVBxos&t=1396), [`23:34`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xafwfGVBxos&t=1414)
- After about a year of consistent videos he got into Google and backed off, announcing it in a video [`23:34`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xafwfGVBxos&t=1414), [`23:55`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xafwfGVBxos&t=1435)
- Counterintuitively, the channel went exponential *after* the "I got into Google" video: it was "the best sales pitch in the world" — proof the method worked. It bothers him that the videos didn't change, only the branding did [`24:12`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xafwfGVBxos&t=1452), [`24:31`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xafwfGVBxos&t=1471)
- Next step: a completely free website that was really just a catalog of the videos — it went viral too [`24:31`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xafwfGVBxos&t=1471)
- Shortly after his Google promotion, he realized he could try NeetCode full-time: with algorithms he could go "super deep" in a way Google's business problems never allowed [`24:53`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xafwfGVBxos&t=1493)
