# Quitting Amazon after two months

Navi's first big tech job — on Amazon's Alexa org — ended with an impulsive resignation two months in.

## Background

- Went to Washington State University (not UW — he wasn't a strong high school student); grinded hard on DSA interview prep and a good GPA to land Amazon [`13:12`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xafwfGVBxos&t=792), [`13:34`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xafwfGVBxos&t=814)
- Was self-aware that people skills were his weakness: fine working alone through documentation, but "working with other people was very, very difficult" [`13:34`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xafwfGVBxos&t=814)

## What went wrong

- The Alexa org was "not a well-oiled machine": lots of manual work, very stressful. A week before he joined, a teammate wrote in the team channel "This feels like a thankless job" [`14:05`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xafwfGVBxos&t=845), [`14:29`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xafwfGVBxos&t=869)
- Five or six new grads joined the same team within one to two weeks, more than doubling a team of ~4 experienced engineers — but deadlines meant nobody actually onboarded them [`15:34`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xafwfGVBxos&t=934), [`15:55`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xafwfGVBxos&t=955)
- Every experienced engineer was committing at 3:00 a.m. with 8–9 a.m. meetings the next morning — not manager-mandated, but implicit: "if you're one person who's not doing it at 3:00 a.m., you're going to be the first in line to maybe get kicked out" [`16:14`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xafwfGVBxos&t=974), [`16:33`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xafwfGVBxos&t=993)
- Host's context: Amazon at the time had a target of 6% unregretted attrition per year, pushing managers toward PIPs and cutthroat dynamics [`16:56`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xafwfGVBxos&t=1016)
- Navi had to submit his resignation three times before it was accepted — he suspects because a voluntary quit counts as *regretted* attrition and looks bad [`17:21`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xafwfGVBxos&t=1041)

## Reflection

- He doesn't blame individuals or hold a grudge — "it was just a crappy situation" and a bad culture fit; in hindsight he thinks he could have survived it [`14:52`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xafwfGVBxos&t=892), [`18:07`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xafwfGVBxos&t=1087)
- The quit was an impulse decision during a period of personal issues; the relief quickly turned into "okay, now what do I do?" [`15:10`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xafwfGVBxos&t=910)
