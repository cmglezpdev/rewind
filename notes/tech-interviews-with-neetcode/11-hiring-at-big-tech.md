# Hiring at Big Tech (and elsewhere)

Current state of the interview bar, the cheating problem, and the formats that actually work at smaller scale.

## The current bar

- Junior-level big tech interviews are still mostly DSA. Anecdotally people say it's getting harder, but from those who pass, US difficulty is roughly unchanged [`47:54`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xafwfGVBxos&t=2874)
- Difficulty varies strongly by country — in India it's "leak code hards and super hards" [`48:18`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xafwfGVBxos&t=2898)

## Cheating and the return to on-sites

- 5–6 years of mostly remote interviews spawned interview-cheating tools; Google has now pretty much returned to on-sites — whiteboard or laptop, but in person with someone watching, so "you're probably not going to be able to cheat your way through that" [`48:37`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xafwfGVBxos&t=2917), [`48:58`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xafwfGVBxos&t=2938)

## Promising alternative formats (small companies)

- Any standardized, scalable process can be gamed; the best signal is watching someone actually work (like an internship) [`49:18`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xafwfGVBxos&t=2958)
- Many small companies run **trial periods** — a few days to a couple of months — though that's infeasible for candidates who already have jobs [`49:43`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xafwfGVBxos&t=2983), [`50:03`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xafwfGVBxos&t=3003)
- Navi's own hiring: work samples similar to the real job plus conversation. "I don't even care about what answer they give me… I care about why did they say that" — can they defend trade-offs, or are they regurgitating a ChatGPT answer? [`50:03`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xafwfGVBxos&t=3003), [`50:27`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xafwfGVBxos&t=3027)
- This format doesn't scale to big tech size — which is why he doesn't expect big tech to adopt it [`51:04`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xafwfGVBxos&t=3064)
- Open-source companies mostly skip the problem: they hire proven contributors (e.g. Dax DMing open-source contributors to work on opencode) — "if you're doing things in public people can get a pretty good understanding of how you work" [`51:27`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xafwfGVBxos&t=3087), [`51:50`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xafwfGVBxos&t=3110)
