# The future of software engineers

Reflecting on Tim O'Reilly's "end of coding as we know it" prediction and what actually changed.

## Change is slower than predicted

- A year after O'Reilly's essay: models got dramatically better, big tech friends code "completely differently now" — yet businesses operate in wait-and-see mode; most people are still employed, doing more work than before [`1:01:46`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xafwfGVBxos&t=3706), [`1:02:08`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xafwfGVBxos&t=3728)
- Companies over-rotated toward AI and are rebalancing — it's still trade-offs, still "move fast and break things" [`1:02:40`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xafwfGVBxos&t=3760)

## What won't go away

- Business judgment, knowing what value to produce, and weighing engineering trade-offs: "how can you have an LLM weigh the trade-offs for you?" [`1:03:06`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xafwfGVBxos&t=3786)
- Host's addition: even a "simple" feature (a bug-report button) hides edge cases, state, user tiers, business domain — all context someone must hold and prioritize even when prompting instead of coding [`1:03:29`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xafwfGVBxos&t=3809), [`1:03:47`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xafwfGVBxos&t=3827)

## Willingness to change as a survival trait

- Microsoft's voluntary buyouts were reportedly offered only to employees of a certain age with 10–15 years at the company — Navi speculates it targets people less willing to learn a completely new way of working; similar to the 2014 layoffs when Satya took over [`1:04:32`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xafwfGVBxos&t=3872), [`1:04:56`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xafwfGVBxos&t=3896), [`1:05:17`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xafwfGVBxos&t=3917)

## No programmer extinction

- Every prior leap (cloud computing, higher-level languages) should have reduced programmer headcount — "it just hasn't happened" [`1:05:43`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xafwfGVBxos&t=3943), [`1:06:11`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xafwfGVBxos&t=3971)
- Counter-current to ever-higher abstraction: DHH/37signals left AWS (a few million dollars/year) for their own infrastructure and saved massively — at a certain scale, rolling your own stack pays [`1:07:09`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xafwfGVBxos&t=4029), [`1:07:33`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xafwfGVBxos&t=4053)
- Engineering adoption is largely culture, sales, and marketing (why MongoDB got big), and it snowballs — then corrects [`1:07:59`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xafwfGVBxos&t=4079), [`1:08:18`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xafwfGVBxos&t=4098)
- Warning: when AI subsidies run out, companies that embraced AI programming may cut back — cost will become "a really big issue" [`1:08:43`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xafwfGVBxos&t=4123)
