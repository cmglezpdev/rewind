# Google vs Amazon

Joining Google months after quitting Amazon was "the opposite experience" — and it shaped how Navi thinks about company culture and promotion.

## Amazon PTSD and the Google reset

- He arrived at Google in "Amazon PTSD mode", having extrapolated his two-month Amazon experience to all of big tech: don't ask questions, don't be friendly, just work intensely [`18:22`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xafwfGVBxos&t=1102), [`18:51`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xafwfGVBxos&t=1131)
- People were friendly; he reciprocated, but was still too scared to ask questions and worked mostly alone on a project that turned out harder than intended [`18:51`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xafwfGVBxos&t=1131)
- Ironic outcome: doing everything solo made his manager and team see him as "independent" — exactly what's needed to go from junior to mid-level. He got promoted in about a year, very uncommon at Google, and only *then* felt comfortable asking questions [`19:14`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xafwfGVBxos&t=1154), [`19:35`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xafwfGVBxos&t=1175), [`21:03`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xafwfGVBxos&t=1263)
- Host's observation: engineers carry ingrained reflexes from previous company cultures, and the industry rarely accounts for how hard adapting is [`20:06`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xafwfGVBxos&t=1206)
- Culture mapping is real: Google Cloud leadership came largely from Amazon; Googlers distrusted Amazon-bred managers (frugality vs. Google perks), and Google slowly drifted that direction with the layoffs [`20:24`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xafwfGVBxos&t=1224), [`20:44`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xafwfGVBxos&t=1244)

## What promotion at Google takes

- Junior → mid-level is mostly about working independently; it gets harder at each level above [`21:03`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xafwfGVBxos&t=1263)
- His formula: put in the work + be reasonably smart (most people are) + **right team, right project** — "you could be a 10x, 100x engineer, and if you're working on relatively easy stuff", there's nothing to prove [`21:25`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xafwfGVBxos&t=1285), [`21:47`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xafwfGVBxos&t=1307)
- Google's documentation culture (metrics, artifacts, design docs for everything) produces too many design docs, but it's a "necessary evil" — otherwise engineers would work on whatever they feel like with no quantifiable business impact [`21:47`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xafwfGVBxos&t=1307), [`22:10`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xafwfGVBxos&t=1330)
