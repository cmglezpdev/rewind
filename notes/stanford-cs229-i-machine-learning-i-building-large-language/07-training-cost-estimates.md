# Training cost estimates

All values below belong to the lecturer's rough Llama 3 405B example. They are historical estimates, not current rental quotes or audited project costs. [`55:07`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9vM4p9NN0Ts&t=3307) [`57:13`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9vM4p9NN0Ts&t=3433)

- The model has about 405 billion parameters. Reading the token figure together with the earlier 15-trillion-token discussion gives approximately 15.6 trillion tokens and about 40 tokens per parameter. The transcript drops "trillion" in the local sentence. [`40:14`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9vM4p9NN0Ts&t=2414) [`55:25`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9vM4p9NN0Ts&t=3325)
- Approximate dense-model training compute is \(C\approx6PD\), where \(P\) is parameter count and \(D\) is training tokens. The example gives roughly \(3.8\times10^{25}\) FLOPs. [`55:50`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9vM4p9NN0Ts&t=3350) [`56:23`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9vM4p9NN0Ts&t=3383)
- With about 16,000 H100 GPUs, the lecturer estimates roughly 70 days or 26 million GPU-hours. He contrasts that with a reported figure of 30 million GPU-hours without establishing why they differ. [`56:46`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9vM4p9NN0Ts&t=3406) [`57:13`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9vM4p9NN0Ts&t=3433)
- At an assumed rental price of $2 per GPU-hour, 26 million GPU-hours cost about $52 million. This is a rental-equivalent calculation. [`57:13`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9vM4p9NN0Ts&t=3433) [`57:36`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9vM4p9NN0Ts&t=3456)
- Assuming 50 employees at $500,000 per year adds $25 million of annual salary expense. Combined with compute, the lecturer rounds to about $75 million. The salary term is annual, so this is a rough project-budget illustration rather than a precise bill for a 70-day run. [`58:00`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9vM4p9NN0Ts&t=3480)
- The lecturer estimates roughly 4,000 tonnes of CO2 equivalent. He does not provide the calculation inputs in the transcript, and his view of its significance is an opinion. [`58:27`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9vM4p9NN0Ts&t=3507) [`58:56`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9vM4p9NN0Ts&t=3536)
- His outlook assumes roughly 10 times more training FLOPs for each new generation, subject to GPU and energy availability. Treat it as a stated industry ambition, not a guaranteed law. [`58:56`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9vM4p9NN0Ts&t=3536) [`59:20`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9vM4p9NN0Ts&t=3560)
