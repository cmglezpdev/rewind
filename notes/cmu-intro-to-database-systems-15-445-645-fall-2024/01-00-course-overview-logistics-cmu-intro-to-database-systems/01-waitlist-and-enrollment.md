# 01 — Waitlist & Enrollment

- The lecture exists to move logistics off the first day of class so day one can go straight into the relational model and relational algebra ([`0:46`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=otE2WvX3XdQ&t=46)).
- The instructor no longer controls the waitlist: the course grew popular enough that separate administrators now run the undergraduate **445** and graduate **645** sections ([`1:39`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=otE2WvX3XdQ&t=99)).
- Emailing the instructor about enrollment does nothing — admissions are negotiated between programs and schools, outside his control ([`2:09`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=otE2WvX3XdQ&t=129)).
- Mitigation: with new database professor **Jignesh Patel** hired in the last year, 445/645 is now offered **every semester**, so students shut out in the fall can take it in the spring ([`2:09`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=otE2WvX3XdQ&t=129), [`2:40`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=otE2WvX3XdQ&t=160)).
- Practical advice: if you are not already enrolled, enrollment is unlikely — start considering other options ([`2:40`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=otE2WvX3XdQ&t=160)).
