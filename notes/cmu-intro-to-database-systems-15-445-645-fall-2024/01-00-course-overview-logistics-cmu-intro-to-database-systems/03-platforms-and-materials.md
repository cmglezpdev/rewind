## Non-CMU students

- A **separate non-CMU Gradescope site** is offered: sign up for an account, use the login code, and set the university to "Carnegie Mellon University" or it won't work ([`6:14`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=otE2WvX3XdQ&t=374), [`6:32`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=otE2WvX3XdQ&t=392)).
- The terms of that access ([`6:32`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=otE2WvX3XdQ&t=392), [`7:00`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=otE2WvX3XdQ&t=420)):
  - Do **not** post solutions on GitHub.
  - Do **not** email the instructors or TAs for help.
  - Use the unofficial community Discord (maintained by someone outside CMU) instead — CMU students and TAs should stay on Piazza.
  - Projects open for grading to non-CMU students only **after** the CMU deadline.

## Textbook

- Assigned readings per lecture; topics not covered by the book get **supplemental readings/notes** ([`7:56`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=otE2WvX3XdQ&t=476)).
- His view: the best database systems textbook available, and the only one still actively maintained ([`7:56`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=otE2WvX3XdQ&t=476)).
