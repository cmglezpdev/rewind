# 02 — Course Scope & Lecture Rules

## What the course is about

- Subject: the **design and implementation of database management systems** — the internals: algorithms, data structures, and components used to process and manage a database ([`4:09`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=otE2WvX3XdQ&t=249)).
- It is **not** a course on using or administering a database. Homework 1 involves writing SQL; after that students build their own database system that supports SQL and relational databases ([`4:09`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=otE2WvX3XdQ&t=249)).
- Alternatives pointed out for students who want the applied side ([`4:09`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=otE2WvX3XdQ&t=249), [`4:58`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=otE2WvX3XdQ&t=298)):
  - Heinz College (Information Systems) — using and administering databases, one course Oracle-based.
  - An SCS software-engineering / web-programming course — building an application on top of a database.

## Lecture rules

- **Interrupt with questions during lecture.** The instructor talks fast when excited about databases; raise your hand whenever a slide or point is unclear ([`2:57`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=otE2WvX3XdQ&t=177)).
- **No lecture questions immediately after class.** In past years students queued up and he repeated himself; asking during class instead gets the answer captured on the recording and improves future years ([`3:25`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=otE2WvX3XdQ&t=205)).
- Don't ask questions that don't concern the rest of the class (e.g. bathroom permission — "you're all adults") ([`3:25`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=otE2WvX3XdQ&t=205)).
- **No blockchain questions.** He considers blockchains a terrible idea for databases; the topic is off the syllabus for the entire semester ([`3:25`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=otE2WvX3XdQ&t=205)).
