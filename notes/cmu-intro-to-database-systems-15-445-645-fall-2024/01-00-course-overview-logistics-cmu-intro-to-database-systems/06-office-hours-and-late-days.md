# 06 — Office Hours & Late Days

## Office hours

- Instructor and TA office hours are posted on the course website, plus a calendar showing time slots through the day ([`13:53`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=otE2WvX3XdQ&t=833)).
- **No Sunday office hours**, even though projects and homeworks are due Sunday — regular hours run Monday–Friday ([`14:08`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=otE2WvX3XdQ&t=848)).
- Rationale: weekday-only hours push students to start projects early instead of the night before ([`14:08`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=otE2WvX3XdQ&t=848)).
- Exception: on the **Saturday before each of the four project deadlines**, a multi-hour "power session" with multiple TAs — the only Saturday office hours of the semester ([`14:08`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=otE2WvX3XdQ&t=848)).

## Late policy

| Rule | Detail |
|---|---|
| Late days | **4**, granted automatically, usable for any reason, projects only ([`11:18`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=otE2WvX3XdQ&t=678), [`14:56`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=otE2WvX3XdQ&t=896)) |
| Homeworks | **No late days** — all-or-nothing ([`14:56`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=otE2WvX3XdQ&t=896)) |
| Past your late days | **-10% per day** late on a project ([`14:56`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=otE2WvX3XdQ&t=896)) |
| Medical / uncontrollable emergencies | Handled case by case; email the instructor ([`11:18`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=otE2WvX3XdQ&t=678), [`14:56`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=otE2WvX3XdQ&t=896)) |
| Job interviews / interview prep | **Not** a sufficient reason; exceptions will not be granted ([`15:26`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=otE2WvX3XdQ&t=926)) |
