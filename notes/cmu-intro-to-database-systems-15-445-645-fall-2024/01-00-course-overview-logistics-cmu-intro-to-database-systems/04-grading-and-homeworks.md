# 04 — Grading Breakdown & Homeworks

## Grade weights

| Component | Weight |
|---|---|
| Homeworks | 15% |
| Projects | 45% |
| Midterm exam | 20% |
| Final exam | 20% |

Source: ([`8:35`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=otE2WvX3XdQ&t=515), [`9:04`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=otE2WvX3XdQ&t=544))

- Projects dominate because the course counts as a **software systems elective** for both grads and undergrads, so it must be heavily project-based ([`8:35`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=otE2WvX3XdQ&t=515)).
- **The class is curved**, but by how much is decided after grades come in. Course difficulty is roughly constant year to year; exams will be tweaked more than usual this year, yet the curve is expected to land about the same ([`9:04`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=otE2WvX3XdQ&t=544)).
- **Extra points** are available by ranking high on the project leaderboards ([`9:04`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=otE2WvX3XdQ&t=544)).

## Homeworks

- **Six homework assignments** across the semester ([`9:37`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=otE2WvX3XdQ&t=577)).
- HW1: writing **SQL queries for DuckDB and SQLite**; every homework after that is pencil-and-paper problem solving ([`9:37`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=otE2WvX3XdQ&t=577)).
- Submitted and **autograded through Gradescope**; solutions released after the deadline ([`9:37`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=otE2WvX3XdQ&t=577)).
- All homework is **individual** — no copying ([`9:37`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=otE2WvX3XdQ&t=577)).
