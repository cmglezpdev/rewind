# 07 — Academic Integrity

- Do not plagiarize on homeworks or projects — stated explicitly every year, and this year on video ([`15:45`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=otE2WvX3XdQ&t=945)).
- **How they catch it**: public GitHub repos from non-CMU followers are visible to the staff too, so those solutions get fed into Gradescope's **plagiarism checker** alongside student submissions ([`7:26`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=otE2WvX3XdQ&t=446), [`15:45`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=otE2WvX3XdQ&t=945)).
- Extra deterrent: the instructor says he will take this recording's timestamp to Warner Hall as proof that students were warned, removing any excuse ([`15:45`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=otE2WvX3XdQ&t=945)).
- Side observation from the leaderboards: **non-CMU submissions are generally not as good as CMU code**, so copying them is a bad bet as well as a violation ([`7:26`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=otE2WvX3XdQ&t=446)).
- Applies to copying from friends and from random internet sources alike; if something is genuinely ambiguous, email the instructor first, and consult CMU's academic integrity policy (linked on the slide) ([`16:24`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=otE2WvX3XdQ&t=984)).
