# 08 — Industry Flash Talks & Recruiting

## Flash talks (new this year)

- Industry partners sponsor course development and research in the CMU database group this semester ([`0:46`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=otE2WvX3XdQ&t=46), [`16:45`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=otE2WvX3XdQ&t=1005)).
- Format: a **10-minute talk at the end of class every Wednesday**, starting around week two, given by a representative, engineer, or founder of a database company ([`16:45`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=otE2WvX3XdQ&t=1005)).
- Content: what their system does, why it's interesting, and which problems it solves ([`17:16`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=otE2WvX3XdQ&t=1036)).
- Purpose: let companies — not just the professor — show how the semester's material maps onto real production problems, reinforcing the lecture topics ([`17:16`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=otE2WvX3XdQ&t=1036)).
- These are explicitly **not recruiting talks**; in-class sessions stay on systems and ideas ([`17:46`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=otE2WvX3XdQ&t=1066)).

## Recruiting event

- Separate on-campus event on **September 16–17**, open to everyone in the class; details to be posted on Piazza a week or two out ([`17:46`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=otE2WvX3XdQ&t=1066)).
- Companies come to campus for internships and full-time roles ([`17:46`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=otE2WvX3XdQ&t=1066)).
- Instead of the generic "submit your resume to the website" pile, companies were asked to set up **dedicated channels, email addresses, and portals for CMU DB students**, so applications reach hiring managers on the database projects directly ([`17:46`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=otE2WvX3XdQ&t=1066), [`18:31`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=otE2WvX3XdQ&t=1111)).
