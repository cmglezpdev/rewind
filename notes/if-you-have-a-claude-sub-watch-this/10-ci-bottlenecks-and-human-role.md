# CI bottlenecks and the human role

- Local verification can overwhelm modest hardware even when agent orchestration is cheap. Parallel Rust builds are his example of a workload that saturates cores. [`1:03:10`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=D8PikZ1KhUo&t=3790)
- His proposed remedies are running CI on GitHub, Blacksmith, Depot, or another server, or queueing jobs so several heavy compilations do not run at once. [`1:03:10`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=D8PikZ1KhUo&t=3790) [`1:03:33`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=D8PikZ1KhUo&t=3813)
- He reports that moving Maria to the Blacksmith CLI let her trigger remote CI and reduced her local RAM constraint. The video gives no before/after memory measurement. [`1:03:33`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=D8PikZ1KhUo&t=3813) [`1:03:55`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=D8PikZ1KhUo&t=3835)
- The human role shifts toward selecting problems, supplying feedback, and improving compute and verification systems. He describes optimizing CI triggers, core usage, and many concurrent attempts on one machine as rewarding engineering work. [`1:04:52`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=D8PikZ1KhUo&t=3892) [`1:05:16`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=D8PikZ1KhUo&t=3916)
- His closing habit is to dispatch tasks during other activities and check results later, including from a phone. He still encounters agent mistakes but spends less time watching them occur. [`1:04:13`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=D8PikZ1KhUo&t=3853) [`1:04:31`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=D8PikZ1KhUo&t=3871)
- He ends by encouraging experimentation while subscription subsidies last. Whether those economics persist is left open rather than established by the video. [`1:06:00`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=D8PikZ1KhUo&t=3960) [`1:06:26`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=D8PikZ1KhUo&t=3986)
