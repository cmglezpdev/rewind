# Subscription economics and API boundaries

All figures here are Theo's estimates or statements at recording time. They are not independently verified prices, allowances, or legal conclusions.

## Reported capacity

- $200 spent on either API buys $200 of metered usage; Theo argues personal subscriptions allow much more inference for internal coding work. [`5:23`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=D8PikZ1KhUo&t=323)
- For a $200 Claude subscription, he estimates about $8,000 of monthly API-equivalent usage, with only $4,000 available to Fable because of its separate 50% allowance. [`6:11`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=D8PikZ1KhUo&t=371)
- He rejects the $100 Claude tier for heavy use: it has half the total allowance but one quarter of the five-hour allowance, making the monthly capacity harder to consume. [`6:31`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=D8PikZ1KhUo&t=391) [`6:51`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=D8PikZ1KhUo&t=411)
- For a $200 Codex subscription, he estimates about $12,000 of inference and says Astra and cheaper models such as Soul share the allowance without a separate model bucket. [`6:51`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=D8PikZ1KhUo&t=411) [`7:07`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=D8PikZ1KhUo&t=427)
- He quotes Fable at $10 per million input tokens and $50 per million output tokens, compared with an earlier Mythos preview offer of $25 input and $125 output. [`7:07`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=D8PikZ1KhUo&t=427) [`7:29`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=D8PikZ1KhUo&t=449) [`7:56`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=D8PikZ1KhUo&t=476)
- His explanation for low prices is competitive pressure and large margins. The roughly 95% margin figure is explicitly rumor and informal calculation, not disclosed company accounting. [`8:12`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=D8PikZ1KhUo&t=492) [`8:31`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=D8PikZ1KhUo&t=511) [`8:53`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=D8PikZ1KhUo&t=533)
- He cites 14 Codex resets in 30 days and extrapolates potential usage to roughly $24,000. He also says new $200 subscriptions were temporarily paused. Both statements describe conditions at filming. [`10:05`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=D8PikZ1KhUo&t=605)
- He favors direct Claude and Codex subscriptions over Cursor, OpenCode, or T3 Chat for this purpose, arguing third-party discounts are much smaller. His suggested 30–50% Cursor discount is speculation. [`15:38`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=D8PikZ1KhUo&t=938) [`16:12`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=D8PikZ1KhUo&t=972) [`16:44`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=D8PikZ1KhUo&t=1004)

## Privacy and permitted use as presented

- Theo recommends disabling "Improve the model for everyone" under data controls and mentions a similar Claude setting. His assertion that this makes personal and team terms effectively identical is his claim, not a legal assessment supplied by these notes. [`11:00`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=D8PikZ1KhUo&t=660) [`11:26`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=D8PikZ1KhUo&t=686)
- He draws a firm boundary around user-facing inference: use the API for public requests. He says subscriptions should support internal coding and experiments, not resale of inference or public traffic. [`11:51`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=D8PikZ1KhUo&t=711) [`12:08`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=D8PikZ1KhUo&t=728)
- His later Opus 5.5 update reduces the need for this elaborate setup: he finds its behavior similar to Fable 5.1 and struggles to exhaust one subscription, so multiple accounts or a proxy may be unnecessary. [`19:43`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=D8PikZ1KhUo&t=1183) [`20:05`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=D8PikZ1KhUo&t=1205)
