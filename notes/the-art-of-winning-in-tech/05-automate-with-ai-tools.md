# Automate repetitive work: reduce the speed tax

## What the speed tax means

- Khader defines the "speed tax" as the time and opportunities lost through a slow, manual workflow. He argues that automation frees effort for the engineer's main goals. [`7:05`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=j7vSphawXyY&t=425) [`8:27`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=j7vSphawXyY&t=507)

## Workplace example

- A fellow junior engineer on the same team reportedly shipped nearly three times as much work, with cleaner and more accurate code. Khader initially attributed the difference to ability. [`5:57`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=j7vSphawXyY&t=357) [`6:18`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=j7vSphawXyY&t=378)
- Khader manually planned, coded, formatted, and added test cases. His colleague used automatic formatting on file save and automatic test generation, before ChatGPT existed. The transcript names no scripts or test-generation method. [`6:42`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=j7vSphawXyY&t=402)
- Khader attributes the colleague's greater output, opportunities, and promotion to the more automated workflow. This is his workplace account, not a controlled productivity comparison. [`7:05`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=j7vSphawXyY&t=425)

## Look beyond code generation

- Text entry: Khader reports 57 words per minute typing versus about 166 using speech input with the tool named "WhisperFlow" in the transcript. He describes this as roughly three times faster and extends the claim to producing three times more work. The numbers measure text-entry rate. [`7:31`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=j7vSphawXyY&t=451) [`8:01`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=j7vSphawXyY&t=481)
- Presentations: he names Gamma and JetBrains as tools that can reduce hours of presentation work to minutes. No worked comparison is provided. [`8:01`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=j7vSphawXyY&t=481)
- Research: he names Perplexity and Notebook LM for finding, organizing, and summarizing information. [`8:01`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=j7vSphawXyY&t=481)
- Audit recurring tasks such as typing, making presentations, and sorting email. Automate where a suitable tool can reduce time spent away from your main engineering goals. [`8:27`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=j7vSphawXyY&t=507)
