## 5. Improve Your AI Coding Workflow

[`9:02`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=j7vSphawXyY&t=542) The fourth lesson in winning in tech is to stop prompting AI and start directing it. Think of AI like an incredibly powerful horse. A horse can be faster and stronger than you will ever be, but if you put someone on top who has no idea where they're going, all of that power becomes useless. The horse might run in the wrong direction, potentially attack people, and a lot of crazy stuff could happen. And that's actually how most people use AI.

[`9:25`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=j7vSphawXyY&t=565) They open up ChatGPT and say, "Build me an app."And then the AI gives them something generic, and then they say, "Make it better."And then the AI might change a few colors. Then they say, "No, make it even better." As if that even does anything. And then ultimately, there's this back and forth loop where AI makes tiny corrections while the human secretly hopes that the AI will read their mind. Then it's kind of like riding a horse, but just screaming random directions at it hoping it'll find the right path.

[`9:49`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=j7vSphawXyY&t=589) And so, if you actually want to win in tech and actually get ahead of most people in AI, you must learn the RPI workflow. And this is the workflow that will elevate AI into becoming your thought partner rather than just a bot. First is R. You've got to make AI research the problem. For example, if you have a task that deals with adding authentication to an application, do not immediately start coding. Instead, tell AI to investigate the proper practices for handling users, security protocols, and databases.

[`10:17`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=j7vSphawXyY&t=617) Then ultimately, it should return relevant files with the short summary of how the system works. Once you do that and review the results, second is P. You've got to make AI create you a plan. The AI should lay out exactly which files need to change, what it'll add to each one, and how every change will be tested. After that, you, as the engineer, need to review that plan before AI starts executing and writing a whole bunch of code.

[`10:42`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=j7vSphawXyY&t=642) Finally, we got I, and this is the time where you let AI implement the plan one step at a time. It'll go and implement step one of the plan, and then you have to go and check through the process. Then it'll do step two, same thing over and over again until AI iteratively implements the whole project. Far too many people just want AI to do everything for them, but you need to start taking control and directing AI if you actually want to get ahead of most people and ultimately win in tech.
