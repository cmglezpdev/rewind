# Learn from experts

## Turn knowledge gaps into a curriculum

- "Be the dumbest person in the room" means seek environments where others know more and approach the gap as a learning opportunity. [`0:31`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=j7vSphawXyY&t=31) [`1:23`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=j7vSphawXyY&t=83)
- Despite studying computer science at Georgia Tech and working as a software engineer, Khader felt unprepared among AI leads, data scientists, and researchers. Terms such as MCP and agent-to-agent protocol exposed gaps in his AI knowledge. [`0:31`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=j7vSphawXyY&t=31) [`0:55`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=j7vSphawXyY&t=55)

## The learning process

1. Ask questions and record unfamiliar terms. Khader's conference notes became his AI curriculum. [`1:23`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=j7vSphawXyY&t=83)
2. Research the questions afterward, including what AI agents are and how generative AI differs from machine learning. [`1:46`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=j7vSphawXyY&t=106)
3. Experiment with tools and build projects to develop practical understanding. [`1:46`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=j7vSphawXyY&t=106)

## Reported outcome

- Khader says he progressed to following conference conversations, asking better questions, and interviewing industry leaders he had previously found intimidating. [`1:46`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=j7vSphawXyY&t=106)
- His recommendation is to prioritize access to people you can learn from over protecting the feeling of being the most knowledgeable person present. [`2:16`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=j7vSphawXyY&t=136)
