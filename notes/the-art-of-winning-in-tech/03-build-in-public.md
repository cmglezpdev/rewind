# Build in public

## Make your process visible

- Khader argues that getting people to care about your work and consider hiring you is a central challenge when AI makes creating coding projects easier. Share projects on LinkedIn and Twitter and invite feedback. [`4:03`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=j7vSphawXyY&t=243)
- Share how you build as well as what you finish. In the example, people could observe the developer's skills and decisions, while feedback revealed what potential users wanted. [`3:05`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=j7vSphawXyY&t=185)

## Mark Lou and ShipFast, as reported by Khader

1. After years of failed tech startups, Lou took a minimum-wage job to support his family. [`2:40`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=j7vSphawXyY&t=160) [`3:05`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=j7vSphawXyY&t=185)
2. He joined a developer community and began sharing his building process online. Even failed projects generated feedback and reasons to follow his progress. [`3:05`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=j7vSphawXyY&t=185)
3. By August 2023, he had about 35,000 followers who knew his story and had seen him build consistently. [`3:34`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=j7vSphawXyY&t=214)
4. ShipFast launched to that existing audience and reportedly earned $6,000 in its first two days, $40,000 in its first month, and later more than $1 million in total revenue. [`3:34`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=j7vSphawXyY&t=214)

## Apply the lesson

- Publish class projects too. Khader reports that a friend received recruiter messages offering an interview by his third machine-learning post. This is an anecdote about visibility, not a guaranteed posting threshold. [`4:28`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=j7vSphawXyY&t=268)
- The proposed mechanism is repeated public evidence of skill leading to feedback, familiarity, and trust before a sale or hiring conversation. [`3:05`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=j7vSphawXyY&t=185) [`3:34`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=j7vSphawXyY&t=214)
