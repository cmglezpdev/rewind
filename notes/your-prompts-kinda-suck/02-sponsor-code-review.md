This section is a Macroscope advertisement. Product benefits are Theo's endorsements. [`1:42`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=m-B3jIMMmUE&t=102)

- The reusable idea is a narrowly scoped review instruction with explicit triggers. The segment does not provide a defect reduction measurement or a complete configuration to reproduce.
