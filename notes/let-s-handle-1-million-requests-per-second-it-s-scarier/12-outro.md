# Outro and project direction

- The uploader frames the video's goal as improving engineering thinking, not teaching a complete production architecture for 1 million requests per second. [`2:35:21`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=W4EwfEU8CGA&t=9321) [`2:35:35`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=W4EwfEU8CGA&t=9335)
- The video intentionally moves away from narrow "how-to" content and toward judgment, creativity, and systems thinking. [`2:35:50`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=W4EwfEU8CGA&t=9350)
- The total AWS bill for the month is about $2,000, including research and other work, with roughly $800 for databases and $1,200 for EC2 compute. [`2:36:15`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=W4EwfEU8CGA&t=9375) [`2:36:31`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=W4EwfEU8CGA&t=9391)
- The uploader says several mistakes added a few hundred dollars to the bill, but the outcome could have been much worse. [`2:36:50`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=W4EwfEU8CGA&t=9410)
- The next open source project is a URL shortener app, with the future challenge of shortening 1 million links per second. [`2:37:06`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=W4EwfEU8CGA&t=9426)
- That future benchmark is expected to be harder because the real product includes business logic, tables, authentication, and encryption. [`2:37:21`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=W4EwfEU8CGA&t=9441)
- The project is still months from that benchmark because Cpeak and the product need more features before release. [`2:37:44`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=W4EwfEU8CGA&t=9464)
- The uploader argues that production-facing work teaches security, performant code design, and engineering judgment more deeply than toy projects. [`2:37:59`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=W4EwfEU8CGA&t=9479) [`2:38:19`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=W4EwfEU8CGA&t=9499)
