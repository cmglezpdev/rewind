# 4. Is a CS Degree Still Worth It?

## The host's framing of the shift

- **2021**: a CS degree "and a pulse" could land a ~**$200K job at Google**, making a ~**$50,000** degree obviously worth it [`6:14`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Ci7ZDQ3m8cc&t=374).
- **2026**: you can spend up to **$200,000** on a bachelor's or master's and still graduate jobless for **6 months** [`6:14`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Ci7ZDQ3m8cc&t=374).

## Eric's answer: it depends on what you expect to extract

**Wrong framing** — walk in, learn some Python, JavaScript, Java, walk out with a job. That is "not the right framing of what you're putting in and what you're expecting to get out" [`6:14`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Ci7ZDQ3m8cc&t=374).

**Right framing** — use the degree for the meta skills [`6:42`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Ci7ZDQ3m8cc&t=402):

- Learning how to approach an **open-ended project**
- **Scoping** it out
- Learning how to **talk to people**
- Learning how to **define a set of requirements**

- College is "a unique opportunity … to have a lot of time to dial in on those meta skills that you wouldn't have otherwise" [`6:42`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Ci7ZDQ3m8cc&t=402).
- Note the alignment: these are exactly the skills that survive in the roles Eric predicts will grow — see [07 — Three Tech Jobs That Will Explode](07-three-tech-jobs-that-will-explode.md).
