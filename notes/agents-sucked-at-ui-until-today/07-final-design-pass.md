- Theo's explanation is that the prompt encodes his own review process: inspect the new screenshot, compare it with the previous version and other products, score it, and repeat. He describes the skill as basic rather than dependent on a hidden design technique. [`25:27`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xq0eB8vxeqk&t=1527) [`25:51`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xq0eB8vxeqk&t=1551)

He considers programmatic scores preferable to the subjective model reviews used for this UI experiment.
