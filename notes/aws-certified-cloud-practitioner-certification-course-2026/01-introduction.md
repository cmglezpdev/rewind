# Introduction

Course and exam logistics for the AWS Certified Cloud Practitioner (CLF-C02): who the certification is for, how to study, and the exact exam format (questions, scoring, duration, validity).

### Course & instructor

- Taught by Andrew Brown (ExamPro), former CTO; has taught AWS, Azure, GCP, Kubernetes, Databricks, Nvidia courses [`0:46`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7HKot-brXFE&t=46)
- Method: lecture material to memorize key info + hands-on labs in your own account + a free practice exam on exampro.co [`0:00`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7HKot-brXFE&t=0)
- Optional paid materials at exampro.co/clfc02 support the course [`0:32`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7HKot-brXFE&t=32)

### What is the Cloud Practitioner certification

- Entry-level AWS certification teaching cloud fundamentals; close look at AWS core services (compute, storage, networking, databases) and a quick look at the vast AWS service catalog [`1:10`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7HKot-brXFE&t=70)
- Course code is **CLF-C02**, commonly called the **CCP**; a changed course code signals a major content refresh [`1:34`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7HKot-brXFE&t=94)
- AWS is the leading cloud service provider, making this arguably the best starting cloud certification regardless of provider [`1:34`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7HKot-brXFE&t=94)

### Who it's for & its value

- Audience: people new to cloud, executive/management/sales roles gathering strategic cloud-adoption info, and senior engineers/architects doing a periodic refresh [`2:05`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7HKot-brXFE&t=125)
- Value: a "bird's eye / 50,000 ft view" of cloud architecture and AWS services — big-picture, strategic thinking [`2:32`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7HKot-brXFE&t=152)
- Not a hard exam; it does NOT validate you can build cloud workloads and is not enough alone for technical implementation jobs — but it is essential content you shouldn't skip on the way to Solutions Architect [`3:06`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7HKot-brXFE&t=186)
- AWS certifications don't validate programming, technical diagramming, or code management; the instructor sneaks some of those job skills into the course [`4:27`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7HKot-brXFE&t=267)

### Certification roadmap

- Cloud Practitioner is the starting point; the AI Practitioner is a second foundational cert but CCP knowledge should come first [`3:38`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7HKot-brXFE&t=218)
- Most common path after CCP: 1) Solutions Architect Associate, 2) CloudOps Engineer, 3) Developer (some swap the last two); most common line is Cloud Practitioner → Solutions Architect → SA Pro [`3:38`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7HKot-brXFE&t=218) [`4:06`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7HKot-brXFE&t=246)

### Study plan

- Study time: ~30 hours for beginners, ~6 hours with experience; ~24 hours average — e.g., 1–2 hours/day for 14 days split between lectures, labs, and practice exams [`4:56`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7HKot-brXFE&t=296) [`5:18`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7HKot-brXFE&t=318)
- To pass: watch lectures, memorize key info, do hands-on labs, take online practice exams (ExamPro's are hand-made, not AI-generated) [`5:18`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7HKot-brXFE&t=318)
- Labs are technically skippable for this cert but strongly recommended to cement knowledge; most people who follow the curriculum pass [`5:40`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7HKot-brXFE&t=340)

### Exam domains

- Four domains: Cloud Concepts; Security & Compliance; Cloud Technology & Services; Billing, Pricing & Support — most of the knowledge is in Cloud Technology & Services [`6:07`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7HKot-brXFE&t=367)

### Exam logistics & scoring

- Taken via Pearson VUE only — online or at a partnered test center; test centers are less stressful because the environment is controlled [`6:07`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7HKot-brXFE&t=367)
- Exams are proctored (supervised); you must show your desk/workspace [`6:37`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7HKot-brXFE&t=397)
- Passing grade: ~70% — aim ~15% higher; scoring 85% on practice exams typically maps to ~75% on the real exam (~10% gap) [`7:03`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7HKot-brXFE&t=423)
- Don't target 100% — just pass and move on (the 80/20 rule) [`7:33`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7HKot-brXFE&t=453)
- **65 questions: 50 scored, 15 unscored**; you can afford ~15 wrong; no penalty for wrong answers, so always answer everything [`7:33`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7HKot-brXFE&t=453)
- Formats: multiple choice and multiple answer; unscored questions exist to detect cheating, trial new questions, calibrate difficulty, and prevent dumps — if a question seems impossibly hard, it may be unscored [`7:58`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7HKot-brXFE&t=478)
- Duration: 90 minutes exam time (~1.5 min/question) but **120 minutes seat time** (instructions, showing the proctor your workspace, NDA, feedback) [`8:21`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7HKot-brXFE&t=501)
- Certification is valid for **36 months (3 years)** before recertification [`8:21`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7HKot-brXFE&t=501)
