# The Benefits of Cloud

Why organizations adopt or migrate to public cloud: AWS's current "Benefits of Cloud" list (a rework of the older Six Advantages) and the instructor's normalized seven advantages that apply across all cloud providers.

### AWS's Benefits of Cloud

- The list: **agility, pay-as-you-go, economy of scale, global reach, security, reliability, high availability, scalability, elasticity** [`1:23:26`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7HKot-brXFE&t=5006)
- This is a rework/expansion of AWS's earlier "**Six Advantages of Cloud**"; the original six now appear as sub-bullets under the new categories [`1:23:52`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7HKot-brXFE&t=5032)
- Newly added items vs the original six: **high availability, elasticity, reliability, and security** [`1:23:52`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7HKot-brXFE&t=5032)
- Instructor's critique: the AWS list is still missing **fault tolerance** (and disaster recovery) [`1:24:46`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7HKot-brXFE&t=5086)
- The cloud-architecture-flavored benefits (HA, scalability, elasticity, etc.) are covered in depth later in the course's Cloud Architecture section [`1:24:20`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7HKot-brXFE&t=5060)

### The instructor's seven advantages (normalized across CSPs)

Derived from teaching fundamentals for all major cloud providers; it maps well onto AWS's new benefits list [`1:25:20`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7HKot-brXFE&t=5120):

1. **Cost-effective** — pay for what you consume, no upfront cost, on-demand / pay-as-you-go (PAYG) pricing, thousands of customers sharing the cost of resources; AWS historically said "on-demand pricing", Azure said "pay as you go" — AWS now uses both terms [`1:25:20`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7HKot-brXFE&t=5120) [`1:25:51`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7HKot-brXFE&t=5151)
2. **Global** — launch workloads anywhere in the world by just choosing a region [`1:26:13`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7HKot-brXFE&t=5173)
3. **Secure** — the provider handles physical security; cloud services can be secure by default, with access configurable to a granular level [`1:26:13`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7HKot-brXFE&t=5173)
4. **Reliable** — data backup, disaster recovery, data replication, fault tolerance [`1:26:13`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7HKot-brXFE&t=5173)
5. **Scalable** — increase or decrease resources and services based on demand [`1:26:13`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7HKot-brXFE&t=5173)
6. **Elastic** — automate scaling during spikes and drops in demand [`1:26:13`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7HKot-brXFE&t=5173)
7. **Current** — underlying hardware and managed software is patched, upgraded, and replaced by the provider without interruption to you (a benefit not on AWS's own list) [`1:26:13`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7HKot-brXFE&t=5173)
