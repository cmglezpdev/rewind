# 7. Sampling, verification, and agent merges

- At high volume, Poteto recommends sampling PRs rather than reviewing every one. Review samples rigorously, identify recurring shortcuts or inefficient patterns, then change the environment so future agents avoid them. [`49:15`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MN9dGgmLyso&t=2955) [`49:37`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MN9dGgmLyso&t=2977) [`50:26`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MN9dGgmLyso&t=3026)
- The goal is not to stop looking at the work. Sampling provides feedback for changing skills, lint rules, constraints, and types; it does not mean treating quality as solved. [`51:04`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MN9dGgmLyso&t=3064) [`51:32`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MN9dGgmLyso&t=3092)
- Poteto says he has more than 10 coordinator agents working across areas, including performance, user-reported bugs, and exploratory rewrites. He reviews their PRs after they land by inspecting commit history, then reverts or changes the environment when needed. [`52:11`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MN9dGgmLyso&t=3131) [`53:32`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MN9dGgmLyso&t=3212)
- In Pstack's full-autopilot mode, each PR can go through verifier agents that run the app, click through it like a user, search for regressions, and ask the implementation agent to fix issues before landing. The process is token-intensive and can be tuned by changing the number of verifier agents. [`52:38`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MN9dGgmLyso&t=3158) [`53:07`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MN9dGgmLyso&t=3187)
- Poteto credits trust in autopilot to the combination of verification and a constrained environment. He describes the transition as scary at first, and says it took sustained work to reach the point where agents could merge while he slept.
