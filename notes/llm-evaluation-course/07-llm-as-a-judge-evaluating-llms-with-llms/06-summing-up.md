# Summing up: strengths and limits

## Why it's powerful

- Works for nuanced criteria that classic metrics can't capture [`5:07`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Qj3u_kzfJVo&t=307)
- Works for live monitoring, where no ground-truth reference answer exists [`5:07`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Qj3u_kzfJVo&t=307)
- Lets you scale the expertise of your domain experts [`5:07`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Qj3u_kzfJVo&t=307)

## Why it's not magic

- It might not work for very complex criteria [`5:07`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Qj3u_kzfJVo&t=307)
- Even simple criteria need work to align the judge with your expectations [`5:21`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Qj3u_kzfJVo&t=321)
- Saying "LLM judges work" or "don't work" is like saying "machine learning works or doesn't work" — it all depends on the use case and how you apply it [`5:21`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Qj3u_kzfJVo&t=321)
