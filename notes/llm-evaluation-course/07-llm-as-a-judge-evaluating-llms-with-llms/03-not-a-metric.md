# LLM-as-a-judge is not a metric

- It is **not a metric** in the sense that precision or recall is — it's an evaluation *method* that approximates your human judgment [`2:09`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Qj3u_kzfJVo&t=129)
- Mental model: it's like an intern you ask to grade responses following certain instructions — and you must actually give those instructions [`2:09`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Qj3u_kzfJVo&t=129)
- With a human intern you'd show examples: "this is a good response", "this is a bad response — and here's why"; an LLM judge works exactly the same way [`2:26`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Qj3u_kzfJVo&t=146)
- Consequence: the quality of the judge lives in the evaluation prompt — you must write a very good one to make the labelling work for your case [`2:26`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Qj3u_kzfJVo&t=146)
