# LLM evals are product-specific

## Every evaluation framework is unique to its product

- Each framework is very **product-specific**: know your user, understand the risks you're protecting against, and have a clear idea of what "good" means for your use case [`5:25`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RIGU1hy3UR8&t=325).
- Evaluations should therefore be designed **together with domain experts** [`5:25`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RIGU1hy3UR8&t=325).
- Evaluations are only **partially a technical problem** (how to set them up and run them through the app); the **contents** of the evaluations matter just as much [`5:25`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RIGU1hy3UR8&t=325).
- Conclusion: evals should be done by the **complete team**, not just engineers [`5:43`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RIGU1hy3UR8&t=343).
