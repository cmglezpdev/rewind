# Introduction & when we need LLM evals

## What the video covers

- Big-picture introduction to evaluating LLM applications: what quality means for LLM-powered apps, the general process for running evaluations, and specific scenarios like regression testing and prompt comparison [`0:02`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RIGU1hy3UR8&t=2).

## The two moments when evaluations matter

1. **Pre-deployment** — while building the product, to make sure it's good enough before shipping [`0:22`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RIGU1hy3UR8&t=22).
2. **In production** — once deployed, to make sure it works as expected in the real world [`0:22`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RIGU1hy3UR8&t=22).

- This video focuses on **pre-deployment evaluation** [`0:22`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RIGU1hy3UR8&t=22).
