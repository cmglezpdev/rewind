# From vibe checks to manual labeling

The evaluation process typically matures in stages as the product evolves.

## Stage 1 — Vibe checks

- The first application version usually gets evaluated informally: try it out, look at the outputs, decide if you like them — what the community calls **vibe checks** [`1:40`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RIGU1hy3UR8&t=100).
- Limitation: not systematic [`1:40`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RIGU1hy3UR8&t=100).

## Stage 2 — Manual labeling / annotation

- A more formal review process: you or someone on your team grades responses and systematically decides if they're good or bad [`1:59`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RIGU1hy3UR8&t=119).
- Best practice: add **annotations explaining each decision** — they later help you design better metrics [`1:59`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RIGU1hy3UR8&t=119).
- Limitation: doesn't scale — you can't grade every response every time, so you need an automated way of labeling responses [`2:14`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RIGU1hy3UR8&t=134).
