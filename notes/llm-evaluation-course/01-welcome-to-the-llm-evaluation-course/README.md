# Welcome to the LLM evaluation course

![Video cover](cover.webp)

*Evidently AI · 1:31 · [Watch on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rHs0sP7b5fM)*

Ninety-second welcome video for Evidently AI's course on LLM evaluations, presented by instructor Elena. It positions the course as a high-level, no-coding introduction for anyone building (or considering building) an LLM-powered product, and walks through the full curriculum: from LLM fundamentals and benchmarks, through product-level evaluation (datasets, test cases, methods and metrics, LLM-as-a-judge, safety, AI agents), to production observability and the business case for evaluations.

## Key takeaways

- The course is a high-level introduction to LLM evaluations: no expertise and no coding required, only a general idea of building products with LLMs inside. [`0:01`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rHs0sP7b5fM&t=1)
- The curriculum spans the full lifecycle: LLM basics and benchmarks, then product evaluation — datasets, test cases, methods and metrics. [`0:30`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rHs0sP7b5fM&t=30)
- Dedicated coverage of LLM-as-a-judge (called "fascinating but slightly controversial"), LLM safety (hallucinations, jailbreaks), evaluating AI agents, and post-launch observability, monitoring, and guardrails. [`0:45`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rHs0sP7b5fM&t=45)
- The course closes with the business reasons to invest in AI evaluations, and includes a bonus video demonstrating how to run LLM evaluations with no code. [`1:09`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rHs0sP7b5fM&t=69)
