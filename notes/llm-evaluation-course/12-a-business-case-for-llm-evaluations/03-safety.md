# 03 — Safety

- In some domains errors are not just frustrating, they are dangerous. [`1:26`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZaOo2dce20A&t=86)
- Examples given:
  - A medical chatbot giving incorrect advice can affect a patient's life. [`1:26`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZaOo2dce20A&t=86)
  - In finance, incorrect advice can get you in trouble with regulators. [`1:26`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZaOo2dce20A&t=86)
- Bottom line: if you are not testing for these failure modes, you are gambling with user safety and, ultimately, with liability. [`1:26`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZaOo2dce20A&t=86)
