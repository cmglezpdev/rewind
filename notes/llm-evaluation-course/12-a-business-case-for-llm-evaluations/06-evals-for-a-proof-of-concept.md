# 06 — Do you need evals for a Proof-of-Concept?

- Common objection: "we don't need evaluations yet, it's just a proof of concept". [`3:11`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZaOo2dce20A&t=191)
- Counter-argument: without evaluations, a proof of concept is "just a glorified demo" — and building demos is already very easy. [`3:11`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZaOo2dce20A&t=191)
- To actually *prove* something with a concept you must test the AI product in limited but realistic conditions. [`3:11`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZaOo2dce20A&t=191)
- A real PoC needs to: handle at least some edge cases, and have a system for knowing how you're measurably improving it. [`3:25`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZaOo2dce20A&t=205)
- Conclusion: even a proof of concept needs evaluations — it is one of the most important parts of it. [`3:25`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZaOo2dce20A&t=205)
