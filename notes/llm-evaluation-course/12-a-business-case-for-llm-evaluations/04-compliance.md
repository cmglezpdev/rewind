# 04 — Compliance

## Why prepare now

- There are already many AI regulations across different parts of the world. [`1:49`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZaOo2dce20A&t=109)
- Even if your product is not classified as high risk today, that can change later. [`1:49`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZaOo2dce20A&t=109)
- Pressure can also come from outside regulators: enterprise customers requiring it, or competitors setting the tone. [`1:49`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZaOo2dce20A&t=109)

## What regulations ask for

- Regulations typically concern the same things evaluations cover: safety testing, the ability to audit how the system works, and production monitoring. [`2:04`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZaOo2dce20A&t=124)
- Building a solid evaluation system now is how you prepare ahead of the regulations. [`2:04`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZaOo2dce20A&t=124)
