# 02 — Quality

- Quality is not abstract: if the product is good, people return, keep using it, and you earn money. [`1:00`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZaOo2dce20A&t=60)
- Curiosity drives initial adoption of AI features, but users only stay if the product is actually good. [`1:00`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZaOo2dce20A&t=60)
- Low-quality products cost more than lost users: they can trigger PR disasters or legal risks — "you don't want to be the next headline". [`1:14`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZaOo2dce20A&t=74)
