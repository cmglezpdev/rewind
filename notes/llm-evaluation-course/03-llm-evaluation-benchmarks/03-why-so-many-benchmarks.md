# Why so many benchmarks?

Three reasons the community keeps producing new benchmarks:

1. **Many skills to evaluate** — there are a lot of distinct capabilities worth testing. [`1:51`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=32zkohJICgc&t=111)
2. **Benchmarks become outdated** — as new models are released, harder, more complex benchmarks are needed. [`1:51`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=32zkohJICgc&t=111)
3. **Training contamination** — models can inadvertently memorize benchmark data during training, so fresh benchmarks are always required. [`1:51`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=32zkohJICgc&t=111)

Caveat that leads into the next topic: benchmarks don't necessarily test what you care about when building an LLM-powered product. [`2:07`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=32zkohJICgc&t=127)
