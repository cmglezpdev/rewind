# Introduction: why LLM safety matters

- The video covers three things: what can go wrong with LLM apps, how to protect them, and how to test for safety. [`0:01`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XZZ6glt0AMA&t=1)
- Quality and risk go hand in hand: an incorrect chatbot response is not just bad UX — it can create legal liability or a PR disaster. [`0:01`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XZZ6glt0AMA&t=1)
- Failures come from two directions: innocent mistakes by the model, and bad actors deliberately trying to break the system. [`0:24`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XZZ6glt0AMA&t=24)
