# Risk assessment

- Protection starts with risk assessment: figure out what exactly your app is exposed to before building any defense. [`2:34`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XZZ6glt0AMA&t=154)
- **Exposure depends on the deployment context**: an internal-only application faces a very different risk profile than a public chatbot anyone can interact with. [`2:49`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XZZ6glt0AMA&t=169)
- Once the risks are known, you build protections around them — the video then presents three lines of defense: [product design](07-product-design-choices.md), [guardrails](08-guardrails.md), and [testing/red-teaming](09-safety-testing-and-red-teaming.md). [`2:49`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XZZ6glt0AMA&t=169)
