# 1. Course recap: why evaluations matter

- The course covered two types of evals [`0:02`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=x9mfMkBWwCY&t=2):
  1. Evaluating **LLM models** themselves.
  2. Evaluating **LLM systems** built with those models — where the course spent most of its time [`0:18`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=x9mfMkBWwCY&t=18).
- Why evaluations are important ("some even say evaluations are surprisingly all you need") [`0:18`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=x9mfMkBWwCY&t=18):
  - They help you move faster.
  - They help you prevent failures.
  - They help you deliver value to users.
- The end goal: a system of **measurable continuous improvements** to your product, which the rest of the video assembles piece by piece [`0:34`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=x9mfMkBWwCY&t=34).
