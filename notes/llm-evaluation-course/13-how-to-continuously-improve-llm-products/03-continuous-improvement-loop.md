# 3. The continuous improvement loop

- The loop is bidirectional [`1:48`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=x9mfMkBWwCY&t=108):
  - **Real user data** improves your tests.
  - **Solid tests** enable smart decisions about your product.
- Bonus: once you accumulate enough **labeled data**, two upgrades become possible:
  1. Build your **own evaluators** — potentially replacing LLM-as-a-judge with smaller fine-tuned models [`1:48`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=x9mfMkBWwCY&t=108).
  2. **Fine-tune your main LLM** to perform even better for your specific use case [`2:04`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=x9mfMkBWwCY&t=124).
