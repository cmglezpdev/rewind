# Plan prices and misleading multipliers

## Plans described in the video

- Free costs $0, Pro $20/month, Max $100/month, and Ultra $200/month. [`3:55`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=y5TEATUDDfc&t=235) [`4:12`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=y5TEATUDDfc&t=252)
- Theo considers Ultra sufficient for most everyday development and some heavier work. Without his automated hill-climbing workloads, he says he struggled to use half of one Ultra account's allowance. This is personal experience, not a workload benchmark. [`4:12`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=y5TEATUDDfc&t=252)

## Why the old labels confused users

- The old 5x and 20x labels referred to five-hour allowances, not every limit. Weekly limits arrived later as usage patterns changed. [`2:37`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=y5TEATUDDfc&t=157)
- Max 20x offered 20 times Pro's five-hour allowance, but its later weekly allowance was 10 times Pro's. Users often assumed 20x applied to both. [`3:34`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=y5TEATUDDfc&t=214)
- Theo reports that Mike, the product lead for Claude subscriptions, apologized for the confusing naming. He praises the new naming and explicit 10x weekly comparison as clearer communication. [`3:05`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=y5TEATUDDfc&t=185) [`3:34`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=y5TEATUDDfc&t=214) [`3:55`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=y5TEATUDDfc&t=235)
