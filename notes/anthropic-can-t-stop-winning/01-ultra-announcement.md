# Ultra announcement and its qualification

- Anthropic introduces the Ultra name for the existing $200/month Max 20x subscription, rather than introducing a more expensive tier. [`0:00`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=y5TEATUDDfc&t=0) [`0:21`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=y5TEATUDDfc&t=21)
- The central change is removal of the five-hour limit for **verified Ultra accounts**. The verification requirement qualifies the headline benefit. [`0:21`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=y5TEATUDDfc&t=21)
- Cheaper plans retain five-hour limits. Theo argues those restrictions hurt lower-tier users more and says their enforcement also affects T3 Code. [`0:43`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=y5TEATUDDfc&t=43)
