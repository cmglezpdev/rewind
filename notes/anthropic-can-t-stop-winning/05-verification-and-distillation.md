# Account verification and distillation concerns

## What verification changes

- The announced removal starts with most existing Ultra subscribers. Newer accounts retain a temporary limit until verification, with an email when the restriction is removed. The stated purpose is fraud and abuse prevention. [`9:34`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=y5TEATUDDfc&t=574)
- Verification happens automatically. Verifying a phone number at checkout is separate and does not remove the limit. [`10:06`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=y5TEATUDDfc&t=606)
- The stated account check is **Settings → Usage**: absence of a current-session bar indicates verification. Theo criticizes the lack of published criteria or detail about the process. [`10:06`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=y5TEATUDDfc&t=606)

## Theo's explanation for the restriction

- Distillation, as discussed here, uses large numbers of Claude requests and responses to train another model. Theo argues that subscriptions provide a cheaper source of those outputs than API access, using his $200 versus $12,000 usage comparison. [`10:33`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=y5TEATUDDfc&t=633)
- He attributes demand for rapid subscription consumption to Chinese labs and other companies seeking distillation data. He argues that removing five-hour limits makes this easier and motivates stricter verification. These are his explanations, not evidence of how Anthropic's verification algorithm works. [`10:59`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=y5TEATUDDfc&t=659)
- His criticism is that vague verification rules also restrict legitimate users who want the advertised Ultra benefit. [`9:34`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=y5TEATUDDfc&t=574) [`10:59`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=y5TEATUDDfc&t=659)
