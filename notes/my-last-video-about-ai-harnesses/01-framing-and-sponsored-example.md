- The sponsor segment presents an agent framework called "Eve" in the transcript. Theo describes using it with Dar to expose T3 Code-related functionality through a Discord bot. [`1:09`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ebILGCoN4UU&t=69) [`1:47`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ebILGCoN4UU&t=107)

- Additional folders configure MCP connections, subagents, and schedules. The intended benefit is reusing the same agent through different channels. [`2:30`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ebILGCoN4UU&t=150)
