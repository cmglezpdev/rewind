# 02 — Dax's Path into Tech

## Early years

- Grew up programming; his dad was a software engineer, which lowered the barrier to entry. Started working out of high school, founded a company that got acqui-hired, then bounced around as a consultant and founded more companies before ~6 years of full-time open source [`7:04`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1VqKUrxR2C8&t=424).
- Minecraft modding was the formative experience: he worked on a modding framework itself, built mods, and ran a ~100-player server where he used mods to create "interesting situations" to observe how people behaved — sandbox design over gameplay [`7:52`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1VqKUrxR2C8&t=472).
- The modding community lived on IRC and included very senior programmers who weren't career-motivated — talented people funneling their energy into Minecraft — and he learned a lot from them in a couple of months [`8:31`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1VqKUrxR2C8&t=511).

## Early startup lessons (Ride Health, ~2017)

- Head of engineering at a transportation/healthcare startup that grew to ~20 people and "ended up in a disaster" — though he met his wife there (she was head of product): "better than a startup exit" [`9:22`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1VqKUrxR2C8&t=562).
- Key takeaway: the "bunch of young people building a startup" stereotype is the exception, not the rule. Everyone was in their 20s, insecure, and it played out as politics and drama. He wouldn't invest in all-young teams [`9:44`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1VqKUrxR2C8&t=584).
- Startups are intimate and intense; if you're not fully developed as a person, insecurities show up at work as conflict. He feels his own brain didn't "settle" until ~26, and the change felt like night and day [`10:56`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1VqKUrxR2C8&t=656).

## Why not big tech

- In the early/mid-2010s big tech was the prestigious path, but he "couldn't get it together" to grind the structured interview process — good programmer, but not willing to study for the competition level required [`11:56`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1VqKUrxR2C8&t=716), [`12:38`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1VqKUrxR2C8&t=758).
